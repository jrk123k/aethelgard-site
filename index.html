The night

About the engagement

Aethelgard is the company of the evening: Gloria Tonante, Orchestra Sinfonica Luminar, and Apex Veloce. It is not a destination. It is the work that filled Teatro Luminar on the nights the house opened.

Teatro Luminar, Milano, inaugurated with Primo Atto — a brief engagement, then the ordinary season. The house does not pretend the run continues. What remains is the document of those nights.

Aethelgard: Live at Teatro Luminar is that document: the complete live album. A limited concert video was issued on Blu-ray and is sold out. The soundtrack is released as a free download.

The company

Principals

  • Diva

    Gloria Tonante

    Voice of Aethelgard on the inaugural nights at Teatro Luminar. The dramatic presence at the center of the work — the line the house was built to carry.

  • Orchestra Sinfonica Luminar

    Maestro Giuliano Rossi

    Conductor of Orchestra Sinfonica Luminar. He held the pit and the architecture of the score for the short engagement — the formal spine of Primo Atto.

  • Apex Veloce

    Lorenzo Vale

    Vale and Apex Veloce supplied the rock engine of Aethelgard — voltage against velvet, on the same stage as the orchestra, for the length of the engagement.
